01 — Who I am

I make a product feel used, then I make it get seen.

I’m Danny. I work across Web3 content, community growth, and AI research. I like getting close to a product, understanding what it actually does, then explaining it in a way people can use. The useful part is the same every time: I touch the thing, I find the sentence a real user would care about, then I stay in the replies.
